# Model Y Legacy — Pearl White Multi-Coat

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the existing Deep Blue
Metallic Legacy sprites as edit targets and the user's Pearl White front-left
photo as a paint/identity reference. These are illustrative dashboard sprites,
not official Tesla renders. Shared blind-spot vehicles are unchanged.

## Prompt specifications

- `park.png`: Change only blue body paint and mirror caps to Pearl White
  Multi-Coat. Preserve the existing elevated front-left camera, Legacy body,
  geometry, framing, silver aero wheels, black panoramic glass, windows, trim
  and separate headlights. Pearl white with soft grey modeling, not flat white.
  Whole vehicle, transparent alpha, no floor, shadow, scenery, labels or watermark.
- `drive.png`: Recolor only blue painted body and mirror caps to Pearl White
  Multi-Coat with soft grey shading. Preserve the rear-centered elevated angle,
  zero yaw, symmetry, proportions, black glass roof/windows, separate red Legacy
  rear lamps, black lower bumper and silver wheels. Front toward top and rear
  bumper toward bottom; no Juniper light bar. Whole vehicle including mirrors,
  transparent alpha, no floor, shadow, scenery, labels or watermark.

Generated transparency is preserved during mechanical resizing into the shared
410 × 307 Park and 122 × 136 Drive canvases. Drive is enlarged approximately
10% to match the Juniper's visible vehicle scale despite its transparent margins.
The Park ground shadow is drawn by
the shared Rive scene, not baked into these sprites.
