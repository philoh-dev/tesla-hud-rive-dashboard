# Model Y Juniper — Quicksilver

Built-in image-generator paint edits of the Stealth Grey Juniper sprites,
guided by the user's Quicksilver reference. Alpha is preserved and the final
assets are mechanically downscaled. Blind-spot cars remain shared/unchanged.

## Final prompt specifications

- `park.png`: Change only painted body and mirror caps to luminous cool-neutral
  Quicksilver metallic: lighter than graphite grey but not white, darker silver
  shading and crisp silver reflections. Reference supplies paint only. Preserve
  the original elevated front-left three-quarter camera, proportions, silhouette,
  multispoke rims, tires, dark glass/roof, black trim and thin front light bar.
- `drive.png`: The same paint edit while preserving the centered elevated rear
  camera, zero yaw/symmetry, proportions, silhouette, mirrors, glazing, wheels,
  black lower bumper and continuous red Juniper rear light bar. Do not copy the
  reference's side angle.

Both prompts require the whole isolated car, small transparent margin, no
clipping, actual transparent alpha, no floor/shadow/environment or labels.
These are illustrative dashboard sprites, not official Tesla artwork.
