
## Reviewed projected blind-spot vehicles

`blind-spot-projected-grey.png` and `blind-spot-projected-red.png` are the approved rear three-quarter sprites, mirrored for the right side. Red includes the body, glass and tires. MYJ SG uses `myj_projection.py` for ground-projected dashes, adjacent lane placement, contact shadows and opacity-only presence transitions. Other vehicle exports retain their existing layout.

`drive-chase.png` is the reviewed centered elevated chase-camera ego sprite, generated by built-in imagegen from the original Stealth Grey drive sprite with the adjacent car as an elevation reference. It targets an approximately 18-degree pitch with more roof/glass depth and a foreshortened rear fascia. The high-resolution alpha image is scaled in Rive to the original 109-unit body width and rear contact plane. The original `drive.png` is retained as a reference.
