# Model Y Juniper — Marine Blue

Built-in image-generator paint edits of the existing Stealth Grey Juniper
sprites. The user's Marine Blue Juniper reference supplies the paint color
only. Alpha is preserved and outputs are mechanically downscaled for the
shared dashboard. Blind-spot cars remain shared and unchanged.

## Final prompt specifications

- `park.png`: Recolor only painted body and mirror caps to deep rich Marine
  Blue metallic, with restrained blue highlights and dark navy shadows; no
  cyan, purple or electric-blue shift. Lock the original elevated front-left
  three-quarter camera, proportions, silhouette, multispoke rims/tires, dark
  glass roof/windows, black trim/intakes and thin full-width front light bar.
  Reference supplies paint only, not camera or wheels. Whole car, small
  transparent margin, no clipping, floor, shadow, environment or annotations.
- `drive.png`: Same Marine Blue paint-only edit, retaining the original
  centered elevated rear camera, zero yaw, symmetry, proportions, silhouette,
  glazing, mirror positions, rims/tires, black lower bumper and full-width red
  Juniper light bar. Do not copy the reference's side angle. Entire car,
  transparent alpha, small margin, no shadow, environment or watermark.

These are illustrative dashboard sprites, not official Tesla renders.
