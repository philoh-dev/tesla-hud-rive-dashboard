# Model Y Legacy — Midnight Silver Metallic

Created with the built-in image-generation tool as paint edits of the existing
Deep Blue Metallic Legacy Park/Drive sprites. The user's front-left photograph
guides the Midnight Silver Metallic paint, not the camera angle. These are
illustrative dashboard sprites, not official Tesla renders. Shared blind-spot
vehicles and dashboard behavior are unchanged.

## Prompt specifications

- `park.png`: Recolor only blue body panels and mirror caps to Midnight Silver
  Metallic: medium-dark graphite silver, subtle cool grey/violet cast and
  realistic metallic highlights; not pale Quicksilver or black. Preserve the
  existing elevated front-left camera, geometry, silhouette, framing, separate
  headlights, black panoramic glass/windows/trim, silver aero wheels and wheel
  positions. Whole vehicle and mirrors, genuine transparent alpha; no floor,
  shadow, scenery, labels or watermark. Do not copy the reference's lower angle.
- `drive.png`: Apply the same paint-only change to the rear-elevated sprite.
  Preserve the exact camera, zero yaw, symmetry, body proportions, framing,
  black glass/windows, separate red Legacy rear lamps with dark internal
  elements, black lower bumper, dark blank plate and silver wheels. Front toward
  top and rear bumper toward bottom. No Juniper light bar, floor, ground shadow,
  scenery, labels or watermark. Whole vehicle and mirrors, transparent alpha.

Generated transparency is retained when mechanically resizing to 410 × 307
Park and 122 × 136 Drive pixels. Drive matches the enlarged Legacy canvas;
Park ground shading is provided by the shared Rive scene, not baked into PNGs.
