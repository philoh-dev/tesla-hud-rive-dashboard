Built-in image-generator paint edits of the Stealth Grey Juniper sprites,
guided by the user's Ultra Red reference. Alpha is preserved and the final
assets are mechanically downscaled. Blind-spot cars remain shared/unchanged.

## Final prompt specifications

- `park.png`: Change only painted body and mirror caps to deep glossy saturated
  Ultra Red metallic cherry red, with bright red highlights and dark wine
  shadows; no orange/pink shift or matte finish. Reference supplies paint only.
  Preserve the elevated front-left three-quarter camera, proportions, silhouette,
  multispoke rims, tires, dark roof/glass, black trim and thin front light bar.
- `drive.png`: The same paint edit preserving the centered elevated rear camera,
  zero yaw/symmetry, proportions, silhouette, mirrors, glazing, wheels, black
  lower bumper and continuous red Juniper rear light bar. Keep lamp contrast
  readable against the red paint; do not copy the reference's side angle.

Both prompts require the whole isolated car, small transparent margin, no
clipping, actual transparent alpha, no floor/shadow/environment or labels.
These are illustrative dashboard sprites, not official Tesla artwork.
